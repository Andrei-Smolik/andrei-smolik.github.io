Andrei Smolik Designer

I am a designer working in Western Australia at the intersection of art, architecture and technology. I create public artworks, architectural installations and spatial experiences that respond to their surroundings and bring a distinctive identity to place.

Collaboration is central to my practice. I work with clients, artists, architects, engineers and fabricators to develop a shared vision and translate it into a compelling, buildable outcome. I value clear communication and early technical dialogue, allowing creative ambition and practical requirements to inform each other throughout a project.

My approach combines creative exploration with computational design. Through parametric modelling and digital fabrication, I explore complex geometries while resolving the details of materials, assembly and construction. My work spans integrated façades, sculptural installations, interactive environments and timber structures.

I’ve contributed to projects for educational campuses, public spaces and commercial developments, drawing inspiration from local character of each site. Alongside commissioned work, teaching and international workshops keep my practice exploratory and open to new methods.

For clients, I bring both visual imagination and technical depth: developing a strong concept, communicating its possibilities and maintaining its intent from design to installation.
